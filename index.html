About

A small software company focused on games.

RDE Games is the public-facing name of Red Dragon Enterprises LLC, focused on bringing board-game experiences to digital platforms and creating original games of its own.

When adapting an existing game, the work is undertaken with the appropriate rights or permission from the rights holder.

Focus

Developing original game concepts, mechanics, and supporting software.

Building tools and digital platforms that support game development and play.
